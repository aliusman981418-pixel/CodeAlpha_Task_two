THE BIG PICTURE

What is phishing?

Phishing is a type of social engineering in which someone pretends to be a trusted person or organization to persuade you to reveal information, open a harmful file, visit a fake website, or approve an action.

Phishing can arrive through email, text messages, social media, phone calls, QR codes, or collaboration tools. Some messages are poorly written, but modern attempts may look professional and personal. Grammar and design alone are not reliable ways to judge whether a message is safe.

The safest response to an unexpected request is to verify it independently before acting.
